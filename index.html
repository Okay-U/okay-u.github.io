Riftcount

A free companion app for the Riftbound trading card game — on iOS and Android.

Download on the App Store

Android release in preparation for Google Play.

What it does

Score tracking

Two- and four-player scoreboard with conquer and hold points, a game timer, undo history, and XP for legends that level up.

Tournaments

Sign in to the Riftbound Locator to see your events, pairings, standings, and to report results from the table.

Cards & decks

Browse and filter the card pool, build decks step by step, import and export lists, and check draw odds.

Event metagame

See what the field played, how each legend performed, and how matchups actually went.

Dice

D6, D8, D12 and D20 for anything the table needs to roll.

Free, no ads

No advertising and no in-app purchases. Your decks and game history stay on your device.
