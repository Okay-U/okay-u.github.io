Riftcount

A free companion app for the Riftbound trading card game, on iOS and Android.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts of our own.

What it does

Score tracking

Two- or four-player scoreboard with conquer and hold points, a game timer, undo history, and XP tracking for legends that level up.

Dice

D6, D8, D12 and D20 for anything the table needs to roll.

Card browsing

A searchable, filterable card catalogue with a detail page for every card.

Deck building

Step-by-step deck builder, decklist import and export, mulligan simulator, draw odds, and a per-deck record of the games you played.

Events

Follow your tournament day from inside the app: your events, round pairings, standings, registering or dropping, and reporting results from the table. Riftcount is a companion to the official organized play platform, not a replacement for it.

Your data stays yours

No analytics, no tracking. Decks, game history and settings live on your device only.

How it works

  1. Score a game Open the app, choose two or four players, tap a tile to add conquer or hold points, start the timer, undo any misclick. When the game ends, save the result to the deck you played.
  2. Build a deck In the Cards tab, search or filter the catalogue and open any card. Add it to a deck, or start the step-by-step builder in the Decks tab. Draw sample hands, check the odds, then export the list as text or import one from a friend.
  3. Tournament day In the Events tab, sign in with your organized play account, open your event, check the pairings to find your table, and report the result when the match is over. Standings update as the round closes.
