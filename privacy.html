Privacy Policy

Riftcount: Score Tracker · Effective September 5, 2026 (replaces the version of June 28, 2026)

Riftcount: Score Tracker ("Riftcount", "the app", "we") is a free companion app for the Riftbound trading card game, made by Okay Ünal and published as Pitopia. The app is built privacy-first: we run no servers of our own, we use no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information.

Summary. Everything you create in the app stays on your device. The app only connects to the internet to download the card catalogue and card errata notes, to search for stores, and when you use the optional Events features. Nothing about you is stored by us on any server.

1. Information stored on your device

Your scores, game timer, dice settings, decks, game history and per-deck records, tile colors, favorite stores and app preferences are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them. If you use a device backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), this data may be included in that backup under Apple's or Google's terms.

2. Card catalogue and card images

The card catalogue and card images are downloaded from riftcodex.com, a community card database, and cached on your device. These requests contain no personal information. We have applied for access to Riot Games' official Riftbound card data API and intend to switch the catalogue to it; this section will be updated when that happens.

3. Card errata notes

Card errata notes are fetched from this website (okay-u.github.io), which is hosted by GitHub Pages. The request contains nothing about you; GitHub may log standard server data such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Events (tournaments), optional

The Events features connect to the Riftbound Locator, the organized play platform operated by UVS Games. To use them you sign in with your existing Riftbound Locator account:

  • Your email and password are sent once, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, directly to the Riftbound Locator to log you in.
  • We never store your password. It is not saved to disk, not logged, and not retained after the login request.
  • When login succeeds, only an opaque session token is kept in secure storage on your device (the Keychain on iOS, encrypted storage on Android) so you stay signed in. Signing out, or deleting the app, removes this token.
  • While signed in, the app fetches your events, pairings, standings and registrations from the Riftbound Locator to display them, and sends the results you report. This information is shown live and is not stored by us.
  • Your Riftbound Locator account is managed by UVS Games, not by us. Their handling of your data is governed by their own privacy policy.

Upcoming change. On September 14, 2026, Riftbound organized play moves from the Riftbound Locator to PlayRiftbound.com, operated by Riot Games, with Riot ID sign-in. Riftcount will update its Events features for this. The intended approach is to open PlayRiftbound.com in an in-app browser, so that your Riot sign-in happens on Riot's own pages and no credentials or session data pass through Riftcount. This section will be updated with that release.

5. Player ratings (eloshowdown)

If you open the player profile or an opponent's rating inside the Events features, the app sends that player's public Riftbound Locator player ID to eloshowdown.com to fetch publicly available community rating data, and may send a store's coordinates to a geocoding service (see section 6) to match the store's community. No credentials or account data are shared. eloshowdown's handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy.

6. Store search and geocoding

When you search for stores by city, the text you type is converted into coordinates by a geocoding service: Apple's geocoding service on iOS, Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io, OpenStreetMap data) on Android. Those coordinates are then used to find nearby stores via the Riftbound Locator. Store coordinates may be reverse-geocoded to display a city name. Riftcount does not request access to your device's location and holds no location permission.

7. No tracking or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We do not build a profile of you.

8. Platform services

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) handle downloads, updates and, if you opted in on your device, crash reports, under their own privacy policies. On iOS, the optional lock screen scoreboard (Live Activity) is rendered locally on your device.

9. Data retention and deletion

We retain no personal data on any server. The only credential the app holds is the Events session token, kept locally in secure storage and removed when you sign out or delete the app. To delete or manage your organized play account, use the platform that operates it.

10. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country, and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because Riftcount stores nothing about you on a server, these requests are normally fulfilled by deleting data on your device or by signing out; you can still contact us at any time about them.
