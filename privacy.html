Privacy Policy

Riftcount: Score Tracker · Effective October 20, 2026 (replaces the version of September 5, 2026)

Riftcount: Score Tracker ("Riftcount", "the app", "we") is a free companion app for the Riftbound trading card game, made by Okay Ünal and published as Pitopia. The app is built privacy-first: we run no servers of our own, we use no analytics, advertising or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information.

Summary. Everything you create in the app stays on your device. The app only connects to the internet to download the card catalogue and card errata notes, to search for stores, and when you use the optional Events features. Nothing about you is stored by us on any server.

1. Information stored on your device

Your scores, game timer, dice settings, decks, game history and per-deck records, tile colors, favorite stores and app preferences are stored locally on your device only. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them. If you use a device backup (for example iCloud or Google backup), this data may be included in that backup under Apple's or Google's terms.

2. Card catalogue and card images

The card catalogue is downloaded from our own site (okay-u.github.io) as one file that we rebuild daily from Riot Games' official Riftbound card gallery, with flavour text from the community database DotGG. Card images and symbols load from Riot's content servers. These requests contain no personal information; the catalogue is cached on your device.

3. Card errata notes

Card errata notes are fetched from this website (okay-u.github.io), which is hosted by GitHub Pages. The request contains nothing about you; GitHub may log standard server data such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Events (tournaments), optional

The Events features connect to PlayRiftbound.com, the organized play platform operated by Riot Games. Signing in happens on Riot's own pages inside an in-app browser that is limited to playriftbound.com and Riot's sign-in domains:

  • Your Riot ID and password are entered on Riot's pages only. Riftcount never sees, stores or logs them.
  • Riot's session cookies stay in the in-app browser's own storage on your device. Signing out on PlayRiftbound, or deleting the app, removes them.
  • To show your events natively (your registrations, pairings, standings, the round clock) and to send the results you report, the app sends requests to playriftbound.com on your behalf, carrying that session the same way the website does. These requests go to Riot only; nothing is sent to us or to any other party. The app identifies itself to Riot with its name and version.
  • Depending on the version, the app can also search events and stores, manage favourite stores and register for or drop from events through the same mechanism, with your own session.
  • The data Riot returns is shown live and cached briefly on your device for display. A small diagnostic log on your device records which operations the app called, when, and whether they succeeded, including Riot's error messages and tournament, game and participant identifiers. It holds no cookies, headers or personal details and can be viewed, shared or cleared under Settings. It never leaves the device unless you share it.
  • Your Riot account is managed by Riot Games, not by us. Riot's handling of your data is governed by Riot's own privacy notice. Pages loaded in the in-app browser are Riot's pages and may load the third-party services Riot uses.

Riftbound Locator (legacy). Until the Locator (operated by UVS Games) stops serving Riftbound events, the "Old" section still offers the previous integration: your email and password are sent once over HTTPS directly to the Locator, only an opaque session token is kept in secure storage, and your Locator events are shown live. That section and this paragraph go away when the Locator does.

5. Player ratings (eloshowdown)

If you open the player profile or an opponent's rating inside the Events features, the app sends that player's public Riftbound Locator player ID (legacy "Old" section only) to eloshowdown.com to fetch publicly available community rating data, and may send a store's coordinates to a geocoding service (see section 6) to match the store's community. No credentials or account data are shared. eloshowdown's handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy.

6. Store search and geocoding

When you search for stores by city, the text you type is converted into coordinates by a geocoding service: Apple's geocoding service on iOS, Photon by komoot (photon.komoot.io, OpenStreetMap data) on Android. Those coordinates are then used to find nearby events and stores via PlayRiftbound (or, in the legacy section, the Riftbound Locator). Store coordinates may be reverse-geocoded to display a city name. Riftcount does not request access to your device's location and holds no location permission.

7. No tracking or analytics

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking SDKs, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We do not build a profile of you.

8. Platform services

Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) handle downloads, updates and, if you opted in on your device, crash reports, under their own privacy policies. On iOS, the optional lock screen scoreboard (Live Activity) is rendered locally on your device.

9. Data retention and deletion

We retain no personal data on any server. The only credential the app holds is the Events session token, kept locally in secure storage and removed when you sign out or delete the app. To delete or manage your organized play account, use the platform that operates it.

10. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your country, and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

11. Your rights

Under the GDPR and similar laws you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because Riftcount stores nothing about you on a server, these requests are normally fulfilled by deleting data on your device or by signing out; you can still contact us at any time about them.
